### Top 5 findings
1. **F22 / F27: most of the committed Must scope has no code.** Only FR-16 (partly) and FR-09 (service layer only, no endpoint) are implemented. Three of the four main flows (AD-1 charge, AD-3 top-up, AD-5 suggestion) and the fault flow (AD-7) have no implementation or test. The architecture diagrams show modules, Redis/BullMQ, Caddy and external clients that do not exist. Under "delivered vs promised" this is the biggest risk.
2. **F06 / F05: the SRS has no testable acceptance criteria and no FR definitions.** Acceptance criteria live in a "Requirements Analysis" document that is not in the repo, and FR-01..FR-21 exist only in the form. FR acceptance ("use-case test scenarios pass") cannot be checked from the repository.
3. **F29: card authorization and classification break stated business rules.** Unlinked cards are accepted, no credit is checked (BR-P18, BR-P23), the free-vend tag is rejected at Authorize, and free vend is classified `free` instead of `unattributed`. The integration test locks in the deviation.
4. **F16: STM-1 has unreachable states and dead ends in code.** `pending`, `stopping` and `failed` can never be entered. `review` has no exit (no operator resolve), and orphaned sessions have no hard-cap exit, so sessions can stay stuck. The console's RemoteStop also bypasses the state machine (F17).
5. **F31 / F30: security and research NFRs cannot be met or measured as built.** There is no TLS (`ws://`), no role-based access, and the console is unauthenticated (NFR-OCPP-06, NFR-SEC-01). The no-reconciliation switch needed for the RQ1 baseline is missing, and the SRS contradicts itself on whether the SteVe run is committed (F03).
