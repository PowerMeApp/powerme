# III. Software Requirement Specification

## 1. Product Overview

PowerMe is one shared, fully localized charging platform for Vietnam's independent (non-VinFast) EV-charging market. Independent operators who import OCPP-compliant DC charging hardware (referred to as "ChargeLink" charge points — technically the D1 and YJ-EU product lines by Jiangxi Ruihua) join PowerMe instead of using the vendor's generic, unlocalized software; each operator manages and sees only its own charge points, sites and tariffs (BR-P11, BR-P16), while drivers use one PowerMe app across every partner operator's charge points. Three coordinated applications are built on a self-owned OCPP 1.6J Charging Station Management System (CSMS): a Driver App (Flutter) to find stations with live connector status, get suggestions ranked by expected time until charged, and start/stop and pay for a session from a prepaid Charging Credit while earning loyalty points; an Operator Dashboard (React) for charge point onboarding, pricing, fault monitoring with charge point uptime and utilisation, and analytics (Should); and an Admin Console for platform-wide user, device, card-registry and system-health management. The CSMS (NestJS/Node.js, ocpp-rpc) is the single system of record: every client talks to it over a committed REST contract (openapi.json); live updates are polled every 5 s, with server-sent events (SSE) planned; billing, e-invoicing and loyalty (WP5) and the status, estimation and suggestion services (WP4) share its database and expose no separate API.

**Positioning:** PowerMe is the shared, Vietnamese-localized charging platform for independent operators running imported OCPP charge points: operators get a complete CSMS, app, payment and e-invoicing stack without building one; drivers get every partner operator's charge points in one app, with live status and suggestions that get them charging soonest.

The system's central technical research question — how reliably the CSMS reconciles transaction and metering data after a WebSocket disconnect between it and a charge point — is a first-class functional requirement at Must level (UC-INT-03: capture, deduplicate, replay detection, reconcile) and, since Review 1, a measured experiment at Must level too (NFR-REC-01: three disconnect scenarios on PowerMe's CSMS); repeating the experiment on SteVe as a control remains Should.

The Business Context Diagram (Figure 1) shows PowerMe Platform as a single black box. Seven external entities exchange information with it directly: three human roles (Driver, Operator, Admin) and four external systems — the Charge Point (the D1/YJ-EU hardware, represented in this project by an OCPP charge-point simulator), the Payment Gateway, a licensed E-Invoice Provider and a Map & Routing Service that returns travel times for station suggestions. Table 1 fixes the system boundary and Table 2 lists the 40 one-way information flows drawn in Figure 1. Each flow is a noun phrase at business level (one meaningful package of information per arrow, e.g. Transaction Record covers the start record, meter readings and stop record of a charging transaction); product names, protocols and internal parts are left out on purpose. Connector reservation (Should) has no flow yet; a Reservation Request flow is added if it enters the build.

Cross-cutting business rules that shape multiple features are summarized here; feature-specific rules are listed with each feature in §3.2–§3.5 and consolidated in the Requirement Appendix (§5.1).

- **BR-P01:** A charging session always ends via OCPP StopTransaction, regardless of billing mode — auto-full sessions are stopped by the charge point itself (BMS cutoff); timed, by-kWh and by-cost sessions are stopped by the CSMS issuing RemoteStopTransaction once the selected limit is reached.

- **BR-P02:** Pricing is time-of-day tiered (Peak / Normal / Off-peak); a session spanning a tier boundary is billed using the correct rate for each portion, on the charge point’s own timestamps of the session (start, stop, meter readings), which are used only after reconciliation has checked them against the CSMS clock and the connection epoch.

- **BR-P03:** VinFast-only stations are hidden by default for a registered non-VinFast vehicle profile, with a visible manual override.

- **BR-P04:** Charging Credit is paid in advance, so a VAT-compliant e-invoice is issued for every successful top-up, for the amount collected, when the gateway confirms payment (Decree 123/2020/ND-CP Art. 9 as amended by Decree 70/2025/ND-CP; Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC). A closed csms_billed session produces a charging statement, not an invoice; a refund of unused credit produces an adjustment invoice (BR-P14).

- **BR-P05:** Session, payment, Charging Credit and driver-identity data are handled as sensitive personal data under the PDPL (in force 1 Jan 2026): role-restricted access, explicit consent at onboarding, retention of billing-relevant records for 10 years and raw OCPP logs/telemetry for 1–2 years (working assumption, pending supervisor sanity-check).

- **BR-P06:** No session starts unless the authorizing idTag’s status is Accepted; a charge-point-started session whose idTag is unknown is still recorded (never dropped) as unattributed for operator review.

- **BR-P07:** Network or pricing-configuration changes on a charge point require an explicit ChangeConfiguration + Reset sequence and communicated downtime.

- **BR-P08 (session classification, ADR-0012):** every session carries an origin (app_qr / rfid / free_vend; hmi_password is reserved and not assigned — a password idTag is handled as an unknown tag or the free-vend tag, BR-P09) and a payment_mode (csms_billed / unattributed), resolved once at session start; billing branches only on payment_mode, so only csms_billed sessions are debited from the driver's Charging Credit and a session is never billed twice. RFID wallet cards are not supported (not in the client's requirements).

- **BR-P09 (standard-station assumption):** where the vendor manuals are silent at the OCPP wire level — the free-vend idTag, the HMI-password idTag, supported configuration keys and measurands — the charge point is assumed to behave as a standard OCPP 1.6J charging station: free vend sends a fixed per-charge-point idTag; the HMI password is charge-point-local; the CSMS discovers supported keys with GetConfiguration at boot. Any vendor DataTransfer payload is logged.

- **BR-P18–P23 (prepaid Charging Credit, ADR-0013):** a csms_billed session starts only if the driver's Charging Credit covers the start minimum, and the CSMS stops it at the cutoff reserve; a shortfall (e.g. after an offline charge point) leaves a negative balance that blocks new sessions until a top-up. The credit pays only for charging sold through PowerMe: no withdrawal, transfer or cash-out, top-ups only through the licensed gateway, refunds only to the original payment source — so it is an advance payment for a service, not an e-wallet (Decree 52/2024/ND-CP).

- **BR-P24–P43 (reservation, status, suggestion and loyalty, ADR-0014 and ADR-0015): reservation rules BR-P24–P31 are Should (a driver may hold one Available connector at a time for a short window; a no-show or late cancellation is charged, never when the charge point or platform is at fault); time left** on a busy connector is always shown as an estimated range, never as a promise; suggestions never include incompatible stations, label out-of-range stations as risky instead of dropping them, and rank by expected time until charged (cost mode and reliability penalty planned after the MVP); status carries its freshness, and uptime comes from the status history; loyalty points are earned only on csms_billed charging, redeemed only against a top-up, expire after 12 months and have no cash value.

#### 3.1.6 Activity Diagrams

The main flows are drawn as activity diagrams at system level, following the lecturer guide (Activity, Sequence and State Machine modelling, Ch. 2): swimlanes are the people (Driver, Operator, Admin), the PowerMe Platform as one black box, and the external systems; actions use business language (no protocol or product names); object nodes show the entity state with the exact database value, so every diagram traces to the state machines (Report 4 §3.1.2) and the sequence diagrams (Report 4 §3.2–§3.6). Each diagram has at most 20 actions and keeps every branch; ⋔ marks a call to another activity diagram. AD-1 charging with the Driver App (UC-CHG-02…04), AD-2 top-up (UC-PAY-01, UC-PAY-03), AD-3 lost connection and reconciliation (UC-INT-03, UC-OPS-05), AD-4 connector reservation (UC-CHG-06, Should), AD-5 station suggestion (UC-CHG-07), AD-6 session started at the charge point (UC-INT-02, UC-INT-03), AD-7 fault, alert and remote reset (UC-CHG-05, UC-OPS-03, UC-OPS-04), AD-8 onboarding a charge point (UC-ADM-03, UC-OPS-01, UC-INT-01) and AD-9 billing exceptions (UC-ADM-08, UC-ACC-02).

Main workflow specifications. Following the supervisor's workflow guide (Workflow: definition and specification, W04, W10), each of the five main workflows committed in BM04 is specified with its trigger, precondition, a 5–7 step happy path, decision points carrying their business-rule codes, exceptions, a postcondition that matches the state machines (Report 4 §3.1.2) and its traceability to FR, use cases, screens, design and test cases (Tables 9a–9e). ### 3.2 Charging Session — Start, Stop & Billing Mode Selection

#### 3.2.1 Start / Stop Session

- **Function trigger:** driver taps "Start" on the Station Detail screen — on an Available connector (or, if reservation is built, one they have reserved) (app-initiated), or authenticates at the charge point with an RFID card, the HMI password, or — where the operator has enabled it — free vend (charge-point-initiated); ends when a billing-mode limit is reached, the driver taps "Stop," or the vehicle's BMS signals cutoff.

- **Function description:** App/QR → the CSMS sends RemoteStartTransaction; the charge point then sends Authorize only if its AuthorizeRemoteTxRequests key is true — the CSMS handles both cases (AC-A01). RFID/password/free-vend sessions are charge-point-initiated: the charge point sends Authorize and/or StartTransaction with its idTag and the CSMS classifies the session (§3.5). Stop paths: charge-point-initiated auto-full, or CSMS-initiated RemoteStopTransaction for timed/kWh/cost (BR-P01). The session survives SuspendedEV/EVSE pauses as one transaction (UC-INT-03). Before sending the start command, the CSMS checks that the driver's Charging Credit covers the start minimum and, if the chosen limit costs more than the credit can pay, refuses the start and offers the largest affordable limit (BR-P18); while the session runs it stops it at the cutoff reserve (§3.4.2).

- **Screen layout:** Start Session and Active Session (§3.1.1, Figure 6); Report 4 will carry the wireframes once WP3’s UI design begins.

- **Function details / business rules:** BR-P01, BR-P06, BR-P08, BR-P18, BR-P19, BR-P23, BR-P29, BR-P34; acceptance criteria AC-B01–AC-B06 (billing modes and payment-mode branching), AC-C01–AC-C05 (Charging Credit), AC-A01–AC-A07 (all session-start methods incl. RFID card, free vend and unknown tag) and AC-R03 (starting on a reserved connector) in Requirements Analysis §2. Workflow: AD-1 (Figure 10, §3.1.6) at system level; protocol messages are mapped in the sequence diagrams of Report 4.

### 3.3 Vehicle-Aware Default Station Filtering

#### 3.3.1 Filter Non-Compatible Stations

- **Function trigger:** automatically whenever the Home — Map & List screen loads, using the driver’s registered vehicle profile.

- **Function description:** PowerMe’s target market is explicitly non-VinFast EV drivers — VinFast charge points are contractually closed to other brands, so showing them by default wastes a driver’s attention on stations they can never use, unlike existing locator apps (EVCS, EV HUB) which list VinFast stations by default and rely on a manual per-search filter.

- **Screen layout:** a visible "Show VinFast stations too" toggle overrides the default exclusion (Home screen).

- **Function details / business rules:** BR-P03; Must (UC-CHG-01, WBS 3.5) — together with the suggestion ranked by expected time until charged (§3.7), one of PowerMe's two driver-facing differentiators against the well-reviewed but VinFast-inclusive-by-default competitor apps identified in Report 1 §3.2.

### 3.4 Prepaid Charging Credit & VAT E-Invoicing

#### 3.4.1 Top Up Charging Credit and Issue E-Invoice

- **Function trigger:** the driver confirms a top-up amount on the Charging Credit & Top Up screen (UC-PAY-01); the credit and e-invoice steps run when VNPay's IPN confirms the payment (§3.1.4 item 12).

- **Function description:** the CSMS creates a top-up order within the limits of BR-P22 and redirects the driver to VNPay's hosted page. On a confirmed IPN it adds the amount to the driver's Charging Credit exactly once (BR-P13) and requests a VAT e-invoice for the amount collected from a licensed Vietnamese e-invoice provider's API (sandbox — MISA meInvoice likely; not a direct tax-authority connection), with seller info (buyer details omitted: drivers are individual consumers, as Decree 123/2020/ND-CP Art. 10 allows; invoices to companies are out of scope), service description (prepayment for EV charging), amount, VAT rate/amount and total, per Decree 123/2020/ND-CP Art. 10. The invoice is issued at top-up because Decree 123/2020/ND-CP Art. 9, as amended by Decree 70/2025/ND-CP, sets the invoice time at the moment of collection when a service is paid for in advance.

- **Screen layout:** Charging Credit & Top Up, Payment Gateway Page and History & E-Invoices (Figure 6); the job is automated; refunds, late payments (payments confirmed after the top-up expired, never credited, BR-P13) and e-invoices that need review are handled by the Admin on the Billing Exceptions screen (Figure 8, UC-ADM-08).

- **Function details / business rules: BR-P04, BR-P05, BR-P13, BR-P14, BR-P21, BR-P22, BR-P44, BR-P51. Charging Credit is an advance payment for the charging service, not an e-wallet (Decree 52/2024/ND-CP): it cannot be withdrawn, transferred or used for anything else, top-ups go only through a licensed gateway, and unused credit is refunded only to the original payment source on account closure (requested by the driver in the app, UC-ACC-02; refund requests are created automatically at closure and failed ones are retried by the Admin, UC-ADM-08), with an adjustment invoice. Who sells the charging and how revenue is shared with station owners is decided by the client company that ordered PowerMe; the system records every payment and session and reports revenue, and does not split or pay out money. Free-vend/unattributed sessions have no payer and are reported as a separate revenue line. Whether invoicing at credit use rather than at top-up is preferable is to be confirmed by WP5 with the e-invoice provider. Loyalty-point redemption at top-up is specified in §3.8. Workflow: AD-2 (Figure 11, §3.1.6) and AD-9 (Figure 18) for refunds.**

#### 3.4.2 Credit Check, Cutoff and Settlement

- **Function trigger:** every csms_billed session start (app or RFID card), every MeterValues sample of a running csms_billed session, and session close.

- **Function description:** at start, the CSMS refuses the session if the driver's available credit is below the start minimum (proposed 50,000 VND) and refuses a limit the credit cannot pay at the highest tariff of the day, offering the largest affordable limit instead (BR-P18); an RFID card is accepted only if it is linked to such a driver (BR-P23). While charging, the CSMS estimates the cost of the energy so far at the highest tariff band in force today and sends RemoteStopTransaction when the credit left after that estimate reaches the cutoff reserve (proposed 10,000 VND, BR-P19); the exact per-band cost is computed at settlement (BR-P45). On close, the final cost is debited as one ledger entry. If it exceeds the credit (the charge point was offline or data arrived late), the balance goes negative and no new csms_billed session can start until a top-up restores it (BR-P20).

- **Screen layout:** Start Session (insufficient-credit prompt), Active Session (remaining credit) and Charging Statement (§3.1.1, Figure 6).

- **Function details / business rules:** BR-P02, BR-P18–BR-P20, BR-P23; acceptance criteria AC-C01–AC-C05 in Requirements Analysis §2.3. The start minimum, cutoff reserve and top-up limits are Admin-configurable.

### 3.5 Session Classification & Anti-Double-Billing

#### 3.5.1 Classify Session Origin and Payment Mode

- **Function trigger:** on Authorize/StartTransaction, for every session, before any pricing.

- **Function description:** the CSMS looks the idTag up in the card registry (UC-ADM-04) and against the charge point’s configured free-vend tag: app-started → origin app_qr, csms_billed; registered RFID card → rfid, csms_billed; free-vend tag → free_vend, unattributed; any other tag (typically a session that ran while the charge point was offline, or the HMI password mode) → recorded, unattributed, with an unknown_id_tag reconciliation record for the operator (UC-OPS-05). If the session arrives after a reconnect it is flagged delivered_offline.

- **Screen layout:** Card Registry (Admin), Unattributed Sessions (Operator); Analytics shows unattributed sessions as a separate line.

- **Function details / business rules:** BR-P06, BR-P08, BR-P09; AC-A05–AC-A07, AC-B06. RFID wallet cards (stored-value cards that pay at the charge point) are not supported, as they are not in the client's requirements (Report 1 LI-4); a card that is not in the registry is handled as an unknown tag. Workflows: AD-6 (Figure 15) for sessions started at the charge point and AD-3 (Figure 12) for a lost connection, both in §3.1.6.

### 3.6 Connector Reservation (Should)

#### 3.6.1 Reserve, Cancel and Expire a Reservation

- **Priority: Should (team meeting 09/10/2026, ADR-0015). The design below is kept so the work can resume after the Must scope; it is not part of the commitment on the registration form.**

- **Function trigger:** the driver taps Reserve on an Available connector (Station Detail or Suggested for You); the reservation ends when the driver starts a session there, cancels, the hold window elapses, or the connector becomes unusable.

- **Function description:** the platform checks BR-P24 (connector Available, online and compatible; credit at least the start minimum; no other active reservation), creates the reservation as active with an expiry time and holds the connector, which every driver sees as Reserved within 5 s (NFR-RES-02). If the charge point reported the OCPP Reservation profile at boot, the CSMS sends ReserveNow and the charge point accepts only that driver's idTag; otherwise — or if the charge point refuses — the CSMS keeps a platform hold, refusing other drivers' remote starts and answering their Authorize with Blocked (BR-P30). One active reservation is allowed per connector and per driver, so of two simultaneous requests exactly one succeeds (AC-R02). The reservation ends in exactly one state: fulfilled (the holder starts on the same charge point in time, BR-P29), cancelled_free, cancelled_late (50% of the no-show fee, BR-P26), expired_no_show (the no-show fee, debited once, BR-P27) or voided (connector faulted, offline or taken, or operator cancelled — no fee, alternatives suggested, BR-P28).

- **Screen layout:** Reserve Connector sheet (fee and free-cancel cut-off shown before confirming; countdown; cancel), Station Detail and Home status badges (Figure 6); Pricing & Promotions for the per-site hold window and fee (Figure 7).

- **Function details / business rules:** BR-P20, BR-P24–BR-P31; acceptance criteria AC-R01–AC-R07 in Requirements Analysis §2.4. Fees are CreditTransaction entries of type reservation_fee; a negative balance blocks new starts (BR-P20). Defaults (15-min hold, 20,000 VND no-show fee, 5-min free-cancel cut-off) are operator-configurable per site and pending owner confirmation. Workflow: AD-4 (Figure 13, §3.1.6).

### 3.7 Live Connector Status & Station Suggestion

#### 3.7.1 Show Live Status, Estimate Time Left and Suggest Stations

- **Function trigger:** the Home — Map & List screen loads or refreshes (status), a connector's status or meter readings change (status and estimate), or the driver opens Suggested for You, optionally entering the current battery % and a target % (suggestion).

- **Function description:** Status — each OCPP connector status is mapped to a driver-facing status: Available; In use (Preparing, Charging, SuspendedEV, SuspendedEVSE); Freeing soon (Finishing — the car is done and waiting to be unplugged); Unavailable (Reserved, Unavailable, Faulted); Offline (no message within the offline threshold); Unknown (reported Available, but out of date or a start or session is still open). Each status carries the time of its last update; after more than two heartbeat intervals without a message it is shown as possibly out of date (BR-P38, BR-P39). Every change is stored as a ConnectorStatusEvent, from which the operator's uptime, utilisation, fault count and failed-start rate are computed (BR-P43).

- **Time left — for a connector in a session the platform uses the first level of a fallback ladder that has data and shows which kind of estimate it is (BR-P40): (1) the session's own limit — timed: end time minus now; kWh or cost: remaining energy ÷ average power over the last 5 minutes; (2) the current SoC from MeterValues with a two-part DC charging curve (near-constant power up to a taper point around 80%, then decreasing power) towards the target (the limit, otherwise 80% for DC), using the battery size from the vehicle profile (default 60 kWh); (3) the median remaining duration of past sessions on that charge point at the same elapsed time; (4) "unknown". The estimate is shown as a range rounded to 5 minutes and never as a promise (BR-P32).**

- **Suggestion (MVP, brief 07/11, ADR-0017) — with a battery % and the driver's location, the station search ranks compatible stations (GET /stations, sort smart). Without an explicit radius it searches 1.5 × the estimated range (battery % × rated range × 0.75). Road distance is the straight-line distance × 1.3, driven at 30 km/h; each station is labelled ok, tight or risky against the range with a 10 km reserve, never dropped. Wait is a fixed 0 / 5 / 30 min for Available / Freeing soon / In use; charging time to the target (at most 80%) uses min(connector kW, vehicle max DC kW) at 85% efficiency; the best compatible connector is used. Stations are ranked by reach, then total time, then distance; Unavailable, Offline and Unknown connectors get no estimate and are listed last; the cost shown is at most the energy × the Operator's highest tariff today (BR-P33, BR-P41). The Map & Routing Service is not used for ranking. Cheapest mode, the wait from session estimates and the reliability penalty (BR-P42) are planned after the MVP. The app shows the top three, each with a plain reason and a Navigate button; navigation is handed to the phone's map app.**

- **Evaluation — on the simulator the estimate is compared with a straight-line baseline (NFR-RES-03) and the suggestion with choosing the nearest compatible station, under quiet, peak and faulty-station loads, measuring the time until charging starts and until the target is reached (NFR-RES-04); results go to Reports 5 and 7.**

- **Screen layout: Home — Map & List (status badges with freshness), Station Detail and Suggested for You (Figure 6); operator Charge Point detail with uptime and utilisation (Figure 7).**

- **Function details / business rules: BR-P03, BR-P32, BR-P33, BR-P38–BR-P43; acceptance criteria AC-R08–AC-R09 (and AC-S01–AC-S06 in Requirements Analysis §2.6); NFR-RES-01 – NFR-RES-04 (§4.2.3). Workflow: AD-5 (Figure 14, §3.1.6).**

### 3.8 Loyalty Points

#### 3.8.1 Earn, View and Redeem Points

- **Function trigger:** a csms_billed session closes (earn); the driver opens Loyalty Points (view) or chooses to redeem on the Charging Credit & Top Up screen (redeem — UC-PAY-03 «extend» UC-PAY-01); a daily job expires points.

- **Function description:** 1 point is earned per 1,000 VND debited for csms_billed charging when the session closes — none on reservation fees or on unattributed sessions (BR-P34). At top-up, 100 points = 1,000 VND off the amount paid, minimum 500 points, at most 50% of the top-up; the credit receives the full top-up amount and the e-invoice is issued for the amount collected, with the points shown as a discount (to be confirmed with the e-invoice provider) (BR-P35). Points expire 12 months after they are earned, oldest first; they cannot be transferred, have no cash value and are forfeited when the account closes (BR-P36). Points earned on a session that is later amended or refunded are reversed by a compensating entry, and points used on a refunded top-up are restored (BR-P37). Rates are Admin-configurable; in Must the defaults are seeded (policy templates UC-ADM-06 stay Should).

- **Screen layout:** Loyalty Points, Charging Credit & Top Up, and Charging Statement (points earned) (Figure 6).

- **Function details / business rules:** BR-P34–BR-P37; acceptance criteria AC-L01–AC-L04 in Requirements Analysis §2.5. Workflow: AD-2 (Figure 11, §3.1.6) and AD-9 (Figure 18) for refunds. Membership tiers stay Should.

| BR-P48 | Replayed transaction messages are matched by transaction id and connection epoch: an exact duplicate is ignored; a replay that conflicts with stored data (for example a stale meterStop) moves the session to review and holds its billing until the Operator resolves it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| BR-P49 | Only a serial registered by the Admin can be onboarded, once, to a site of the Operator's own; onboarding issues one credential per Charge Point, shown once and stored only as a hash; a connection with a missing or wrong credential is rejected; 5 failed logins within 60 s for one serial from one IP (or 20 from one IP) lock that source for 15 min (HTTP 429).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| BR-P50 | A Charge Point with no message for longer than 900 s is shown Offline to drivers and operators, and its Operator receives an alert.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| BR-P51 | A refund is recorded at once (balance, points, top-up status) and the transfer to the payment source is tracked as a refund request: requested → done or failed. Top-up and late-payment refunds are requested by the Admin; account-closure refunds are created automatically at closure. A refund request is never sent twice: when the result is unknown, the gateway is queried before any resend. It is tried up to 4 times in total (first attempt, then after about 5 min, 30 min and 2 h); a rejected request or a 4th failure becomes failed, the Admin is notified and may retry it one attempt at a time. The adjustment invoice is issued only when the refund is done.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |

### 5.2 Common Requirements

All screens follow the report template’s common conventions: English-language UI text at source level, localized to Vietnamese for the Driver App (its primary audience); toast-style confirmation messages for successful actions; inline red-text validation errors for required-field omissions; consistent date/time display in Vietnam’s local timezone (ICT, UTC+7) with underlying storage in UTC; money stored as integer VND and energy as integer Wh.
