# III. Software Requirement Specification

## 1. Product Overview

PowerMe is one shared, fully localized charging platform for Vietnam's independent (non-VinFast) EV-charging market. Independent operators who import OCPP-compliant DC charging hardware (referred to as "ChargeLink" pillars — technically the D1 and YJ-EU product lines by Jiangxi Ruihua) join PowerMe instead of using the vendor's generic, unlocalized software; each operator manages and sees only its own pillars, sites and tariffs (BR-P11, BR-P16), while drivers use one PowerMe app across every partner operator's pillars. Three coordinated applications are built on a self-owned OCPP 1.6J Charging Station Management System (CSMS): a Driver App (Flutter) to find stations with live connector status, get suggestions ranked by expected time until charged, and start/stop and pay for a session from a prepaid Charging Credit while earning loyalty points; an Operator Dashboard (React) for pillar onboarding, pricing, fault monitoring with pillar uptime and utilisation, and analytics (Should); and an Admin Console for platform-wide user, device, card-registry and system-health management. The CSMS (NestJS/Node.js, ocpp-rpc) is the single system of record: every client talks to it over a committed REST contract (openapi.json) and Socket.IO events; billing, e-invoicing and loyalty (WP5) and the status, estimation and suggestion services (WP4) share its database and expose no separate API.

**Positioning:** PowerMe is the shared, Vietnamese-localized charging platform for independent operators running imported OCPP pillars: operators get a complete CSMS, app, payment and e-invoicing stack without building one; drivers get every partner operator's pillars in one app, with live status and suggestions that get them charging soonest.

The system's central technical research question — how reliably the CSMS reconciles transaction and metering data after a WebSocket disconnect between it and a pillar — is a first-class functional requirement at Must level (UC-INT-03: capture, deduplicate, replay detection, reconcile) and, since Review 1, a measured experiment at Must level too (NFR-REC-01: three disconnect scenarios on PowerMe's CSMS); repeating the experiment on SteVe as a control remains Should.

The Business Context Diagram (Figure 1) shows PowerMe Platform as a single black box. Seven external entities exchange information with it directly: three human roles (Driver, Operator, Admin) and four external systems — the Charging Pillar (the D1/YJ-EU hardware, represented in this project by an OCPP charge-point simulator), the Payment Gateway (VNPay), a licensed E-Invoice Provider and a Map & Routing Service (Goong or Google Distance Matrix) that returns travel times for station suggestions. Table 1 fixes the system boundary and Table 2 lists every information flow drawn in Figure 1. Flows are grouped at business level (one meaningful package of information per arrow, e.g. Transaction Record covers the start record, meter readings and stop record of a charging transaction; Remote Command & Configuration includes the request for state-of-charge readings and, if reservation (Should) is built, the connector hold).

Cross-cutting business rules that shape multiple features are summarized here; feature-specific rules are listed with each feature in §3.2–§3.5 and consolidated in the Requirement Appendix (§5.1).

- **BR-P01:** A charging session always ends via OCPP StopTransaction, regardless of billing mode — auto-full sessions are stopped by the pillar itself (BMS cutoff); timed, by-kWh and by-cost sessions are stopped by the CSMS issuing RemoteStopTransaction once the selected limit is reached.

- **BR-P02:** Pricing is time-of-day tiered (Sharp / Peak / Shoulder / Off-peak); a session spanning a tier boundary is billed using the correct rate for each portion — the CSMS clock is the reference (NTP-synced), never the pillar’s.

- **BR-P03:** VinFast-only stations are hidden by default for a registered non-VinFast vehicle profile, with a visible manual override.

- **BR-P04:** Charging Credit is paid in advance, so a VAT-compliant e-invoice is issued for every successful top-up, for the amount collected, when the gateway confirms payment (Decree 123/2020/ND-CP Art. 9 as amended by Decree 70/2025/ND-CP; Circular 78/2021/TT-BTC). A closed csms_billed session produces a charging statement, not an invoice; a refund of unused credit produces an adjustment invoice (BR-P14).

- **BR-P05:** Session, payment, Charging Credit and driver-identity data are handled as sensitive personal data under the PDPL (in force 1 Jan 2026): role-restricted access, explicit consent at onboarding, retention of billing-relevant records for 10 years and raw OCPP logs/telemetry for 1–2 years (working assumption, pending supervisor sanity-check).

- **BR-P06:** No session starts unless the authorizing idTag’s status is Accepted; a pillar-started session whose idTag is unknown is still recorded (never dropped) as unattributed for operator review.

- **BR-P07:** Network or pricing-configuration changes on a pillar require an explicit ChangeConfiguration + Reset sequence and communicated downtime.

- **BR-P08 (session classification, ADR-0012):** every session carries an origin (app / rfid_online / rfid_wallet / hmi_password / free_vend) and a payment_mode (csms_billed / station_wallet / unattributed), resolved once at session start; billing branches only on payment_mode, so a session already paid at the pillar (wallet card) is priced for reconciliation only and never debited from the driver's Charging Credit.

- **BR-P09 (standard-station assumption):** where the vendor manuals are silent at the OCPP wire level — wallet-card balance reporting, the free-vend idTag, the HMI-password idTag, supported configuration keys and measurands — the pillar is assumed to behave as a standard OCPP 1.6J charging station: balances stay on the pillar and are not reported; free vend sends a fixed per-pillar idTag; the HMI password is pillar-local; the CSMS discovers supported keys with GetConfiguration at boot. Any vendor DataTransfer payload is logged.

- **BR-P18–P23 (prepaid Charging Credit, ADR-0013):** a csms_billed session starts only if the driver's Charging Credit covers the start minimum, and the CSMS stops it at the cutoff reserve; a shortfall (e.g. after an offline pillar) leaves a negative balance that blocks new sessions until a top-up. The credit pays only for charging sold through PowerMe: no withdrawal, transfer or cash-out, top-ups only through the licensed gateway, refunds only to the original payment source — so it is an advance payment for a service, not an e-wallet (Decree 52/2024/ND-CP).

- **BR-P24–P43 (reservation, status, suggestion and loyalty, ADR-0014 and ADR-0015): reservation rules BR-P24–P31 are Should (a driver may hold one Available connector at a time for a short window; a no-show or late cancellation is charged, never when the pillar or platform is at fault); time left** on a busy connector is always shown as an estimated range, never as a promise; suggestions never include incompatible, faulted, offline or out-of-range stations, and rank by expected time until charged or by cost with a reliability penalty; status carries its freshness, and uptime comes from the status history; loyalty points are earned only on csms_billed charging, redeemed only against a top-up, expire after 12 months and have no cash value.

### 3.2 Charging Session — Start, Stop & Billing Mode Selection

#### 3.2.1 Start / Stop Session

- **Function trigger:** driver taps "Start" on the Station Detail screen — on an Available connector (or, if reservation is built, one they have reserved) (app-initiated), or authenticates at the pillar with an RFID card, the HMI password, or — where the operator has enabled it — free vend (pillar-initiated); ends when a billing-mode limit is reached, the driver taps "Stop," or the vehicle's BMS signals cutoff.

- **Function description:** App/QR → the CSMS sends RemoteStartTransaction; the pillar then sends Authorize only if its AuthorizeRemoteTxRequests key is true — the CSMS handles both cases (AC-A01). RFID/password/free-vend sessions are pillar-initiated: the pillar sends Authorize and/or StartTransaction with its idTag and the CSMS classifies the session (§3.5). Stop paths: pillar-initiated auto-full, or CSMS-initiated RemoteStopTransaction for timed/kWh/cost (BR-P01). The session survives SuspendedEV/EVSE pauses as one transaction (UC-INT-03). Before sending the start command, the CSMS checks that the driver's Charging Credit covers the start minimum and caps the chosen limit at what the credit can pay; while the session runs it stops it at the cutoff reserve (§3.4.2).

- **Screen layout:** Start Session and Active Session (§3.1.1, Figure 9); Report 4 will carry the wireframes once WP3’s UI design begins.

- **Function details / business rules:** BR-P01, BR-P06, BR-P08, BR-P18, BR-P19, BR-P23, BR-P29, BR-P34; acceptance criteria AC-B01–AC-B06 (billing modes and payment-mode branching), AC-C01–AC-C05 (Charging Credit), AC-A01–AC-A07 (all session-start methods incl. wallet card, free vend and unknown tag) and AC-R03 (starting on a reserved connector) in Requirements Analysis §2. ### 3.3 Vehicle-Aware Default Station Filtering

#### 3.3.1 Filter Non-Compatible Stations

- **Function trigger:** automatically whenever the Home — Map & List screen loads, using the driver’s registered vehicle profile.

- **Function description:** PowerMe’s target market is explicitly non-VinFast EV drivers — VinFast pillars are contractually closed to other brands, so showing them by default wastes a driver’s attention on stations they can never use, unlike existing locator apps (EVCS, EV HUB) which list VinFast stations by default and rely on a manual per-search filter.

- **Screen layout:** a visible "Show VinFast stations too" toggle overrides the default exclusion (Home screen).

### 3.4 Prepaid Charging Credit & VAT E-Invoicing

#### 3.4.1 Top Up Charging Credit and Issue E-Invoice

- **Function trigger:** the driver confirms a top-up amount on the Charging Credit & Top Up screen (UC-PAY-01); the credit and e-invoice steps run when VNPay's IPN confirms the payment (§3.1.4 item 12).

- **Function description:** the CSMS creates a top-up order within the limits of BR-P22 and redirects the driver to VNPay's hosted page. On a confirmed IPN it adds the amount to the driver's Charging Credit exactly once (BR-P13) and requests a VAT e-invoice for the amount collected from a licensed Vietnamese e-invoice provider's API (sandbox — MISA meInvoice likely; not a direct tax-authority connection), with seller info (buyer details omitted: drivers are individual consumers, as Decree 123/2020/ND-CP Art. 10 allows; invoices to companies are out of scope), service description (prepayment for EV charging), amount, VAT rate/amount and total, per Decree 123/2020/ND-CP Art. 10. The invoice is issued at top-up because Decree 123/2020/ND-CP Art. 9, as amended by Decree 70/2025/ND-CP, sets the invoice time at the moment of collection when a service is paid for in advance.

- **Screen layout:** Charging Credit & Top Up, VNPay Payment (hosted) and History & e-Invoices (Figure 9); the job is automated; refunds, late payments and e-invoices that need review are handled by the Admin on the Billing Exceptions screen (Figure 11, UC-ADM-08).

- **Function details / business rules: BR-P04, BR-P05, BR-P13, BR-P14, BR-P21, BR-P22. Charging Credit is an advance payment for PowerMe's own charging service, not an e-wallet (Decree 52/2024/ND-CP): it cannot be withdrawn, transferred or used for anything else, top-ups go only through a licensed gateway, and unused credit is refunded only to the original payment source on account closure (requested by the driver in the app, UC-ACC-02; the refund is executed by the Admin, UC-ADM-08), with an adjustment invoice. PowerMe is assumed to sell all charging in its own name and settle with site operators (seller of record). Sessions paid by wallet card at the pillar (station_wallet) are not invoiced by the CSMS; free-vend/unattributed sessions have no payer and are reported as a separate revenue line. Whether invoicing at credit use rather than at top-up is preferable is to be confirmed by WP5 with the e-invoice provider. Loyalty-point redemption at top-up is specified in §3.8. #### 3.4.2 Credit Check, Cutoff and Settlement

- **Function trigger:** every csms_billed session start (app or online card), every MeterValues sample of a running csms_billed session, and session close.

- **Function description:** at start, the CSMS refuses the session if the driver's available credit is below the start minimum (proposed 50,000 VND) and caps a timed, kWh or cost limit at what the credit can pay (BR-P18); an online card is accepted only if it is linked to such a driver (BR-P23). While charging, the running cost per tariff band is compared with the credit, and the CSMS sends RemoteStopTransaction when the remaining credit reaches the cutoff reserve (proposed 10,000 VND, BR-P19). On close, the final cost is debited as one ledger entry. If it exceeds the credit (the pillar was offline or data arrived late), the balance goes negative and no new csms_billed session can start until a top-up restores it (BR-P20).

- **Screen layout:** Start Session (insufficient-credit prompt), Active Session (remaining credit) and Charging Statement (§3.1.1, Figure 9).

### 3.5 Session Classification & Anti-Double-Billing

### 3.6 Connector Reservation (Should)

#### 3.6.1 Reserve, Cancel and Expire a Reservation

- **Priority: Should (team meeting 09/10/2026, ADR-0015). The design below is kept so the work can resume after the Must scope; it is not part of the commitment on the registration form.**

- **Function trigger:** the driver taps Reserve on an Available connector (Station Detail or Suggested for You); the reservation ends when the driver starts a session there, cancels, the hold window elapses, or the connector becomes unusable.

Fees are CreditTransaction entries of type reservation_fee; a negative balance blocks new starts (BR-P20). ### 3.7 Live Connector Status & Station Suggestion

#### 3.7.1 Show Live Status, Estimate Time Left and Suggest Stations

- **Function trigger:** the Home — Map & List screen loads or refreshes (status), a connector's status or meter readings change (status and estimate), or the driver opens Suggested for You, optionally entering the current battery %, a target % and the Fastest / Cheapest mode (suggestion).

- **Function description:** Status — each OCPP connector status is mapped to a driver-facing status: Available; In use (Preparing, Charging, SuspendedEV, SuspendedEVSE); Freeing soon (Finishing — the car is done and waiting to be unplugged); Unavailable (Reserved, Unavailable, Faulted); Offline (no message within the offline threshold). Each status carries the time of its last update; after more than two heartbeat intervals without a message it is shown as possibly out of date (BR-P38, BR-P39). Every change is stored as a ConnectorStatusEvent, from which the operator's uptime, utilisation, fault count and failed-start rate are computed (BR-P43).

- **Time left — for a connector in a session the platform uses the first level of a fallback ladder that has data and shows which kind of estimate it is (BR-P40): (1) the session's own limit — timed: end time minus now; kWh or cost: remaining energy ÷ average power over the last 5 minutes; (2) the current SoC from MeterValues with a two-part DC charging curve (near-constant power up to a taper point around 80%, then decreasing power) towards the target (the limit, otherwise 80% for DC), using the battery size from the vehicle profile (default 60 kWh); (3) the median remaining duration of past sessions on that pillar at the same elapsed time; (4) "unknown". The estimate is shown as a range rounded to 5 minutes and never as a promise (BR-P32).**

- **Suggestion — the platform takes compatible stations within the search radius that are not Faulted or Offline (radius capped at battery % × rated range × 0.8 when a battery level is entered), gets travel times from the Map & Routing Service (cached for 2 minutes; straight-line distance ÷ 30 km/h if the service fails), and works out when each station's earliest connector is free (now, or now + estimate). It adds the charging time to the driver's target at min(pillar rated kW, vehicle max DC kW) and the estimated cost at the tariff band in force on arrival. Fastest mode ranks by travel + wait + charging time; Cheapest mode ranks by estimated cost, then time. A station with 7-day uptime below 80%, an open "reported issue", a stale status or a low-confidence estimate gets +10 minutes and a visible label (BR-P33, BR-P41, BR-P42). It returns the top three, each with a plain reason and a Navigate button; navigation is handed to the phone's map app.**

- **Evaluation — on the simulator the estimate is compared with a straight-line baseline (NFR-RES-03) and the suggestion with choosing the nearest compatible station, under quiet, peak and faulty-station loads, measuring the time until charging starts and until the target is reached (NFR-RES-04); results go to Reports 5 and 7.**

- **Screen layout: Home — Map & List (status badges with freshness), Station Detail and Suggested for You (Figure 9); operator Pillar detail with uptime and utilisation (Figure 10).**

- **Function details / business rules: BR-P03, BR-P32, BR-P33, BR-P38–BR-P43; acceptance criteria AC-R08–AC-R09 (and AC-S01–AC-S06 in Requirements Analysis §2.6); NFR-RES-01 – NFR-RES-04 (§4.2.3). Workflow: Figure 16 (AD-5).**

### 3.8 Loyalty Points

#### 3.8.1 Earn, View and Redeem Points

- **Function trigger:** a csms_billed session closes (earn); the driver opens Loyalty Points (view) or chooses to redeem on the Charging Credit & Top Up screen (redeem — UC-PAY-03 «extend» UC-PAY-01); a daily job expires points.

- **Function description:** 1 point is earned per 1,000 VND debited for csms_billed charging when the session closes — none on reservation fees or on station_wallet / unattributed sessions (BR-P34). At top-up, 100 points = 1,000 VND off the amount paid, minimum 500 points, at most 50% of the top-up; the credit receives the full top-up amount and the e-invoice is issued for the amount collected, with the points shown as a discount (to be confirmed with the e-invoice provider) (BR-P35). Points expire 12 months after they are earned, oldest first; they cannot be transferred, have no cash value and are forfeited when the account closes (BR-P36). Points earned on a session that is later amended or refunded are reversed by a compensating entry, and points used on a refunded top-up are restored (BR-P37). Rates are Admin-configurable; in Must the defaults are seeded (policy templates UC-ADM-06 stay Should).

### 4.2 Quality Attributes

#### 4.2.1 Usability

The Driver App’s core session flow (top up → find → start → monitor → statement) targets a UX as close as possible to VinFast’s own tightly integrated in-ecosystem app — PowerMe’s structural disadvantage is not owning the vehicle, so responsiveness and minimal round-trips are treated as a requirement: Socket.IO push over polling for live status, client-side caching of the station list with live availability refresh, and a QR-first low-friction session start. The consent step at onboarding is explicit but single-screen.

**Measurable targets:** NFR-US-01 — from QR scan to "session started" shown in the app ≤ 10 s at p95 on staging with the simulator; NFR-US-02 — a first-time driver completes registration, including consent and vehicle, in ≤ 3 screens.

#### 4.2.3 Performance

Live status and MeterValues updates reach the Driver App / Dashboard via push within NFR-OCPP-02’s bound; the Home screen renders its cached station list immediately with availability refreshing in the background; concurrency is bounded by NFR-OCPP-05’s measured hypothesis rather than a production-fleet figure — a known, stated scope simplification.

**Measurable targets (ADR-0014, ADR-0015): NFR-RES-01 — a station suggestion returns in ≤ 2 s at p95 on staging with 50 simulated stations; NFR-RES-02 — a connector's status change appears on the station map and list within ≤ 5 s at p95, and on the Operator Dashboard and the driver's active-session screen within ≤ 3 s at p95 (NFR-OCPP-02); NFR-RES-03 (Must, measured) — for ≥ 70% of simulated sessions, the time-left estimate taken at 50% of the session falls within its displayed range, reported in Report 5 together with the error of a straight-line baseline; NFR-RES-04 (Must, measured) — the suggestion is evaluated against the nearest compatible station on at least 200 simulated requests per load scenario (quiet, peak, faulty station), reporting time until charging starts and until the target is reached; the commitment is to run and report the comparison (RQ2, hypothesis H2), not a specific improvement.**
